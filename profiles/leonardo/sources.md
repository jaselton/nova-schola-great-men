# Leonardo da Vinci: Primary Sources & Quotations

---

## Teaching Note

Use these source anchors to keep lessons historically grounded. Present notebook quotations as translated excerpts from Leonardo's surviving manuscripts, not as polished public speeches.

---

## Key Quotations

> "The eye, which is called the window of the soul, is the principal means by which the central sense can most completely and abundantly appreciate the infinite works of nature."

**Teaching use:** Leonardo's method begins with trained attention. The eye is not passive; it must be educated.

> "I am fully conscious that, not being a literary man... my subjects are to be dealt with by experience rather than by words."

**Teaching use:** This shows both his insecurity about formal education and his confidence in empirical observation.

> "There is no certainty in sciences where mathematics cannot be applied."

**Teaching use:** Leonardo's observation was not random curiosity. He wanted measured, ordered knowledge.

---

## Source Anchors

- Leonardo's surviving notebooks and codices, especially anatomical, optical, hydraulic, and artistic studies.
- Giorgio Vasari, *Lives of the Most Excellent Painters, Sculptors, and Architects*.
- Martin Kemp, *Leonardo da Vinci: The Marvellous Works of Nature and Man*.
- Carmen C. Bambach, *Leonardo da Vinci Rediscovered*.
