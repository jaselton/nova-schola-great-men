# Leonardo da Vinci: Discussion Prompts by Age

---

## Ages 8-10

1. Why did Leonardo keep looking at ordinary things for so long?
2. What is something you could observe more carefully this week?
3. Why does drawing help you see?
4. Was Leonardo's notebook more like a toy, a school, or a tool? Why?
5. What should you do when you cannot learn in the usual way?

---

## Ages 11-14

1. Was Leonardo's lack of formal education mostly a disadvantage or an advantage?
2. When is experience better than books? When are books better than experience?
3. Why did Leonardo have trouble finishing projects?
4. How did the workshop function like a school?
5. What is the difference between curiosity and scattered attention?

---

## Ages 15-18

1. Can empirical observation produce systematic knowledge without formal theory?
2. How did Leonardo's workshop education shape his epistemology?
3. Was Leonardo's integration across disciplines a strength, a weakness, or both?
4. What structures might have helped Leonardo complete more work?
5. Compare Leonardo's "disciple of experience" posture with scholastic or humanist education.
