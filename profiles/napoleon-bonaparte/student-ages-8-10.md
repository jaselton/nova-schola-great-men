# Napoleon Bonaparte: Student Lesson (Ages 8-10)
## The Soldier Who Practiced Hard

---

## Meet Napoleon

Napoleon Bonaparte was born on the island of Corsica. When he was a boy, he went to military school in France.

He was not the richest boy. He was not the most comfortable boy. But he studied hard. He learned mathematics, maps, cannons, and military discipline.

When France went through a revolution, many old leaders lost power. Napoleon rose because he was brave, clever, organized, and ready to work.

He became a famous general. Later he became ruler of France and then emperor. He helped make laws and schools. He wanted talented people to be able to rise by ability, not just by birth.

---

## The Hard Part

Napoleon did many impressive things, but he also wanted too much power.

He did not only want to serve France. He wanted to command more and more. He made himself emperor, controlled what people could say, and led many wars.

At first, he won many battles. But finally his ambition became too large. His army suffered terribly in Russia. Later he was defeated at Waterloo and sent far away to an island called St. Helena.

---

## What to Imitate

**Practice**  
Napoleon studied and trained carefully.

**Focus**  
He looked for the most important point and acted there.

**Energy**  
He worked hard and moved quickly.

**Order**  
He cared about laws, schools, and good administration.

---

## What to Guard Against

**Pride**  
Being good at something can make a person stop listening.

**Wanting too much control**  
A leader should not treat other people as tools.

**Loving glory too much**  
Winning is not the same as being good.

**Ignoring limits**  
Even strong people cannot do everything.

---

## Memory Sentence

> Talent must obey truth.

Copy this sentence three times in your best handwriting.

---

## Activity: Practice Like a Cadet

Choose one small skill:
- Neat handwriting
- Mental math
- Drawing a map
- Reciting a poem
- Practicing an instrument

Practice for 15 minutes. Then write:

1. What did I practice?
2. What improved?
3. What was still hard?
4. How can practice make talent stronger?

---

## Discussion Questions

1. Where was Napoleon born?
2. What did he study as a young officer?
3. Why did he rise so quickly?
4. Why was his ambition dangerous?
5. What is one skill you can discipline this week?

---

## Closing Lesson

Napoleon shows that practice, courage, and focus can make a person powerful.

But power must obey truth. A great talent becomes dangerous when pride takes command.
