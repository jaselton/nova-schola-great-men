# One-Week Lesson Plan: Anselm of Canterbury

---

## Week Goal

Students will understand Anselm as a model of faith seeking understanding: a formed mind, a prayerful thinker, and a conscience strong enough to resist pressure.

---

## Day 1 — The Search for a Teacher

**Read:** README one-minute story and formation signature  
**Discuss:** Why did Anselm leave home to seek wisdom?  
**Activity:** Make a "formation map" of people, books, habits, and places shaping the student.  
**Output:** One paragraph: "The kind of teacher I need."

---

## Day 2 — Faith Seeking Understanding

**Read:** Age-appropriate student lesson  
**Discuss:** Can questions come from love rather than doubt?  
**Activity:** Serious Question Journal  
**Output:** One carefully written question and why it matters.

---

## Day 3 — Prayer and Logic

**Read:** A short selection from the *Proslogion* opening, with parent help  
**Discuss:** Where does Anselm sound like he is praying? Where does he sound like he is reasoning?  
**Activity:** Mark prayer words and reasoning words in different colors.  
**Output:** Three sentences explaining how prayer and thought work together.

---

## Day 4 — Conscience and Kings

**Read:** Research essay section on refusing royal control  
**Discuss:** What should a person do when power demands too much?  
**Activity:** Role-play Anselm and the king.  
**Output:** A short speech: "Why I cannot surrender this principle."

---

## Day 5 — Firmness and Stubbornness

**Read:** Failures and recovery section  
**Discuss:** When does courage become inflexibility?  
**Activity:** Make a two-column chart: Firm / Stubborn.  
**Output:** One example from Anselm and one example from the student's life.

---

## Optional Weekend Extension

**Essay:** "Can asking hard questions make faith stronger?"  
**Seminar:** Compare Anselm with Augustine or Boethius.  
**Creative:** Draw Anselm's mountain of understanding, labeling each stage: teacher, prayer, study, question, courage, truth.

---

## Assessment

A student understands Anselm if they can explain:

- What "faith seeking understanding" means
- Why Bec and Lanfranc mattered
- Why Anselm resisted royal control
- One virtue to imitate
- One risk to avoid
