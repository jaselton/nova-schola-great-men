# One-Week Lesson Plan: Thomas Aquinas

---

## Week Goal

Students will understand Thomas Aquinas as a model of ordered wisdom: humble intelligence, fair argument, disciplined study, and reverence before mystery.

---

## Day 1 — The Quiet Student

**Read:** README one-minute story and formation signature  
**Discuss:** Why did others underestimate Thomas?  
**Activity:** Make a "hidden strengths" list: abilities others may not notice immediately.  
**Output:** One paragraph: "Why quiet does not mean empty."

---

## Day 2 — Vocation Under Pressure

**Read:** Age-appropriate student lesson  
**Discuss:** Why did Thomas choose the Dominicans even when his family resisted?  
**Activity:** Draw two paths: the impressive path and the faithful path.  
**Output:** Three sentences on the difference between status and vocation.

---

## Day 3 — Fair Objections

**Read:** A short excerpt from *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 1, a. 1, with parent help  
**Discuss:** Why does Thomas list objections before answering?  
**Activity:** Mark the question, objection, "I answer that," and reply in different colors.  
**Output:** One fair objection and one careful reply.

---

## Day 4 — Faith and Reason

**Read:** Research essay section on faith and reason  
**Discuss:** Can truth discovered by reason conflict with truth revealed by God? What if people misunderstand one or both?  
**Activity:** Make a two-column chart: What reason can discover / What revelation teaches.  
**Output:** A short explanation of how Thomas avoids both fear and gullibility.

---

## Day 5 — Systems and Mystery

**Read:** Research essay section on Thomas stopping before mystery  
**Discuss:** Why would a great thinker stop writing?  
**Activity:** Build a small concept map of Thomas's world: God, creation, human action, Christ, sacraments, wisdom.  
**Output:** One paragraph: "Why a system is useful but not ultimate."

---

## Optional Weekend Extension

**Essay:** "Can rigorous thought be an act of humility?"  
**Seminar:** Compare Thomas Aquinas with Anselm or Aristotle.  
**Creative:** Draw Thomas's study desk with four labels: question, objection, answer, mystery.

---

## Assessment

A student understands Thomas Aquinas if they can explain:

- Why Thomas was underestimated
- How vocation required courage
- What the scholastic method does
- How faith and reason relate in Thomas's work
- One virtue to imitate
- One risk to avoid
